Lessons from reviewer feedback (generation 1):
L1. Always verify the invoice addressee matches the client before processing any payment.
L2. Never pay an invoice that contains mathematically inconsistent amounts or totals.
L3. Flag and escalate any invoice demanding same-day payment or threatening collection without client confirmation.
L4. Do not hold routine invoices from known suppliers with matching bank details and correct totals.
L5. Reject any invoice that does not align with the client's delegated payment authority or expected vendor relationships.
